Bones and muscles are BodyParts3D scans (right side). Cartilage is offset from the bone surfaces; the articular disc and the three ligaments are procedural. Opening is modelled as one rotation about the lingula, which approximates the condyle's combined hinge-and-glide forward onto the eminence.

Bones and muscles marked ◆ are derived from BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), licensed under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan (lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d; mirror github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D). Modified: cropped, decimated/smoothed, re-oriented and scaled; this adapted model is shared under the same CC BY-SA licence.

All 26 foot bones, the distal tibia/fibula, leg and foot muscles and the long plantar ligament are BodyParts3D scans. Deltoid, lateral collateral, syndesmotic and spring ligaments and the plantar fascia are procedural. The talocrural joint moves the talus; the subtalar slider moves the rest of the foot about an oblique axis. Toes do not bend separately.

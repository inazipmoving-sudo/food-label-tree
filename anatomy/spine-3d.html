Vertebrae C1–L5, the sacrum, the annuli of the discs, the rib heads and the right-side deep back muscles are BodyParts3D scans. Facet cartilage is offset from the facing bone surfaces. The coccyx, nuclei pulposi, all ligaments, the spinal cord, cauda equina and nerve roots are procedural. Motion is spread across the segments in realistic regional proportions: flexion mostly cervical and lumbar, side-bending mostly thoracic and cervical, rotation concentrated at C1–C2 and the thoracic spine with little in the lumbar spine. Discs are blended between their two vertebrae and the muscles are weighted per vertex to their nearest vertebra.

Bones and muscles marked ◆ are derived from BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), licensed under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan (lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d; mirror github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D). Modified: cropped, decimated/smoothed, re-oriented and scaled; this adapted model is shared under the same CC BY-SA licence.

All 27 hand bones, the forearm and hand muscles, the flexor retinaculum and interosseous membrane are BodyParts3D scans. Collateral and radiocarpal ligaments and the TFCC are procedural. Muscles and tendons are skinned per vertex to the nearest bone segment (forearm, carpus/metacarpals, each phalanx), blended across the wrist, MCP, PIP and DIP joints, so tendons follow the fingers when you make a fist.

Rendering: three.js (MIT).

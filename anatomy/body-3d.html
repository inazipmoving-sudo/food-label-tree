About / Credits

Bones and muscles marked ◆ are derived from BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), licensed under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan (lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d; mirror github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D). Modified: cropped, decimated/smoothed, re-oriented and scaled; this adapted model is shared under the same CC BY-SA licence.

Nervous system: brain and optic pathway from BodyParts3D; spinal cord, cauda equina, spinal roots & ganglia, 11 more cranial nerves with their branches (I, III–XII), brachial plexus roots/trunks/divisions/posterior cord and all arm nerves, intercostal, lumbar-plexus, sacral-plexus, pudendal nerves and the sympathetic trunk/ganglia are meshes from Z-Anatomy (CC BY-SA 4.0, derived from BodyParts3D; cranial nerves partly from University of Dundee CAHID, CC BY 4.0). Items marked ✦ (cervical plexus and its branches, phrenic nerve, lateral and medial cords of the brachial plexus) are not in any open mesh set and are hand-routed tubes – approximate paths. Male / Female switch (top of the panel) swaps in a complete body of each sex; your other checkbox choices are kept, and the female files load only the first time you switch. Male body: one adult male BodyParts3D scan (DBCLS, CC BY-SA 2.1 JP): skin (including the skin of the penis and scrotum), muscles, male pelvis, penis (corpora cavernosa, corpus spongiosum, glans), testes, epididymides, deferent ducts, seminal vesicles, prostate. Female body: skin, pelvis (hip bones, sacrum & coccyx), uterus, cervix, cervicovaginal junction, ovaries, uterine tubes and breasts (fat, lobes, ducts, Cooper's ligaments, nipple & areola) are HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas Visible Human Female models (K. Browne, H. Schlehlein et al., NIH OT2OD026671; CC BY 4.0). Adapted, so approximate: the female skin is the HRA female body rescaled and with its arms and legs re-posed (rotated about shoulder and hip) to sit on this skeleton; the HRA skin has a simplified head and hands. Female muscles are the male muscles warped toward the female skin surface (deep muscles move little). No open meshes exist for the vagina or vulva, so the vagina, labia majora and minora, clitoris (glans, hood, body, crura), vestibular bulbs, vestibule/introitus and Bartholin glands are hand-modelled simplified shapes (marked without ◆), placed from the female pelvis and skin for educational orientation – not scans. Shared by both sexes (one male scan): the rest of the skeleton, organs, vessels, nerves, head hair and eyebrows. Meshes are decimated, and left/right pairs are merged into one checkbox. Each system loads as its own file, skeleton first.

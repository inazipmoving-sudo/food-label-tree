How these were chosen

Searches of OpenAlex for papers about aging together with DNA damage, DNA repair, somatic mutation, epigenetic clocks, telomeres, genome instability, clonal hematopoiesis or mitochondrial DNA; each candidate was screened by hand for being about DNA or genome mechanisms of aging. Impact score = 2 × log2(1 + citations) + journal tier + news/press mentions (up to 3). Citation counts are as of 28 Oct 2026 and grow fast for recent papers, so the ranking favours older papers. Synopses are written from each paper's published abstract; they never add findings that are not in the source.

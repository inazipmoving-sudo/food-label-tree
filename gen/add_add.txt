  Orange B|Synthetic azo dye (US only)|No nutritional role|Not naturally in foods; hot dog and sausage casings
  Potassium bromate|Flour improver (US; banned in EU, UK, California from 2027)|Possible carcinogen|Not naturally in foods; some breads and rolls

  Benzoyl peroxide (flour bleaching)|Flour bleaching agent (US)|Leaves benzoic acid|Not naturally in foods; bleached flour, whey
  Calcium peroxide|Dough conditioner (US)|No nutritional role|Not naturally in foods; bread
  Chlorine dioxide|Flour bleaching agent (US)|No nutritional role|Not naturally in foods; bleached flour
  Propylparaben|Paraben preservative (US; removed from EU list 2006)|Possible endocrine effects studied|Not naturally in foods; tortillas, baked goods
  Allulose|Rare sugar sweetener (US; not authorized in EU)|Few calories|Figs, raisins (traces); low-sugar products
  Monk fruit extract (mogrosides)|Luo han guo sweetener (US GRAS; not EU-authorized)|Sweetness without calories|Monk fruit (luo han guo)
  Mineral oil (food-grade)|Petroleum-derived oil used as release agent|Not absorbed; laxative|Not naturally in foods; candy, dried fruit
  Brominated vegetable oil (BVO)|Citrus-drink emulsifier (US authorization revoked 2024)|Bromine accumulates in tissue|Not naturally in foods; formerly in citrus sodas

  Glucose syrup|Liquid starch hydrolysate|Sugar source|Not naturally in foods; candy, baked goods

  Maple syrup|Concentrated maple sap (mostly sucrose)|Sugar source|Sugar maple sap
  Agave syrup|Fructose-rich syrup|Sugar source|Agave plant

  Gelatin|Animal collagen protein|Gelling agent; protein|Bones and skin of animals; gummies, marshmallows
  Yeast|Saccharomyces cerevisiae|Leavening; B vitamins|Bread, beer, wine

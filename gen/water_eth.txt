  Natural mineral water|Groundwater bottled at source with stable natural minerals|Hydration; adds calcium, magnesium, bicarbonate|Bottled mineral waters
  Spring water|Groundwater bottled at source|Hydration|Bottled spring waters
  Carbonated (sparkling) water|Water with dissolved carbon dioxide|Hydration; may ease feelings of fullness or indigestion|Sparkling and soda water, seltzer, soft drinks
  Purified / distilled water|Water with minerals removed (distillation, reverse osmosis)|Hydration; no mineral contribution|Bottled purified water, ingredient water in drinks
  Water in foods|Moisture naturally present in foods|Supplies roughly 20% of daily water intake|Cucumber, lettuce, watermelon (over 90% water), soups, fruit
  Added water (ingredient)|Water listed as a label ingredient|Hydration; dilutes energy density|Drinks, soups, sauces, bread, cured and injected meats

  Alcohol in fermented foods|Small amounts formed by fermentation|Negligible in normal amounts|Kombucha, kefir, ripe fruit, fruit juice, sourdough before baking

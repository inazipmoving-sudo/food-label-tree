Key facts

  • 9 kcal per gram for all fats, whether saturated, unsaturated or trans (protein and carbs give 4 kcal/g).
  • Melting: straight chains (saturated, trans) pack tightly and are solid at room temperature. Kinked cis chains (unsaturated) are liquid. Longer chains melt higher.
  • Body fat is mostly triglycerides, and most of its fatty acids are unsaturated, with oleic acid the single most common.
  • Firmer near the cooler skin, softer deeper in: fat gets firmer as it cools. The body also tends to keep its outer fat layers slightly more unsaturated so they don't harden.
